**Bizdooroo** (the “Developer,” “we,” or “us”) explains how **Focus Stopwatch (초집중타이머)** (the “Service”) handles information below.

## 1. The Service and Information Handling

The Service measures focus and rest time without requiring an account. Timer and preference information is stored on your device and is not sent to the Developer's servers. You do not need to provide your name, contact details, or account information. Emails you voluntarily send to support are handled as described in Section 5.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device and Its Purposes

| Information | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Accumulated focus and rest time, running status, current mode, and last save time | Restore timer state and elapsed time. |
| Selected preset, current phase, completed rounds, and next-round prompt state | Maintain focus/rest sequences and repeated rounds. |
| Timer names you enter, language, and theme preferences | Preserve your chosen names and display preferences. |

## 3. Permissions and External Services

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo access. It does not use advertising, user tracking, or external analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and has no feature that shares, outsources the processing of, or transfers timer and preference information overseas. If you separately use an email app or external website, that service's policy applies.

## 4. Retention and Deletion

Timers and preferences are kept in the app's storage on your device. The app's reset control resets timer progress; it does not erase all saved names, language, theme, or other preferences. Information is retained until you delete it.

You can remove on-device information by clearing app data where the operating system supports it or by deleting the app. Offloading an app on iOS may retain its data and is different from deleting it. Copies created by operating-system backup and restore features must be managed separately in the relevant backup settings; deleting the app does not by itself delete those copies.

## 5. Support Information and Your Rights

If you contact us by email, we process your sender email address, message, and attachments only to respond and resolve your inquiry. Under the existing policy, we retain this information for one year and then delete it. Contacting support is optional and is not required to use the app. Please do not send unnecessary personal information or information about other people. Email providers are used to transmit and store correspondence.

You may contact us below to request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of processing of your inquiry information, or to withdraw consent. A legal guardian or authorized representative may also make a request; we verify their authority only to the extent necessary. Provide only the minimum information needed to locate the inquiry, such as the sender email address and approximate date. We explain the outcome or any applicable limitations in accordance with applicable law. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete emails you have already sent.

We cannot remotely access, correct, or delete information stored only on your device. Please manage it through the app and operating-system controls. We delete inquiry information when it is no longer needed; if retention is legally required, we retain it on the applicable legal basis for the required period.
