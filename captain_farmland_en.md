**Bizdooroo (비즈두루)** (the “Developer,” “we,” or “us”) explains the information processed by **Captain Farmland (농지대장, captain_farmland)** (the “Service”) and its purposes below.

## 1. The Service and Information Handling

The Service is a farm management game where you expand farmland and research and build crops and facilities. No registration or login is required, and game progress is stored on your device. Gameplay does not require server communications. The app has no feature that sends game or user information to the Developer or external servers, and does not ask for names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or other identity information.

| Information | Location and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Game currency, donation medals, donation counts, and accumulated discounts | Stored in the device's SQLite database to maintain progress. Donations are an in-game feature. |
| Owned land, crop/facility placement, research status, current map, and unlocked maps | Stored in the device's SQLite database to restore the farm and progress on each map. |
| Last save/harvest time, play time, touch acceleration level, and fever status | Processed in the device's SQLite database for gameplay and offline earnings. |
| Quiz order and last question, tutorial completion/skip status | Stored in the local database and preferences to manage quizzes and tutorial display. |
| Language, progress display, and tile animation preferences | Stored in device preferences to preserve display choices. |
| Sender email address and inquiry contents | Processed to respond and resolve issues if you contact us by email. |

## 2. Permissions and External Features

The Service does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo access. It has no QR code scanning, external-app sharing, or social login features. If you separately use an email app or browser to contact us or visit a support website, that service's policy applies.

## 3. Retention and Deletion

1. Game progress and preferences remain on your device until you delete or reset them. The Developer has no feature for storing or restoring game information on a server.
2. The full reset in app settings resets the game database and removes tutorial completion/skip records. It does not remove language and display preferences.
3. To remove all information, clear app data where the operating system supports it or delete the app. Offloading an app on iOS may retain data and is different from deleting it. Operating-system backup and restore copies must be managed separately in the relevant backup settings; deleting the app does not by itself delete those copies.
4. Inquiry emails are retained for one year to respond and resolve issues, and are then deleted. If retention is legally required, we retain them on the applicable legal basis for the required period. Deleting the app or resetting the game does not delete emails you have already sent.

## 4. Service Providers and Safeguards

The app has no advertising, analytics, or external crash-reporting SDKs, and no in-app payment feature. In-game purchases and donations use game currency and progress.

Game data is processed in the app's storage on your device. The app has no feature that shares game data with external servers, outsources its processing, or transfers it overseas. The Developer cannot remotely access, correct, or delete game data on your device.
