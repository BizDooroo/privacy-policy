The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or similar details. It has no sign-up or login, ads, usage analytics tools, error reporting tools or in-app purchases. The story and the mini-games all run on your device, and the app does not connect to the internet.

## 1. Service and Information Processed

This app is a picture-book story game: you read the winter story of a grandmother and her granddaughter Suji, with mini-games between the scenes.

| Information processed | Where it is processed and why |
| --- | --- |
| Your place in the story (Continue), the chapters you have reached, and the choices you made in the story | Kept only in the app's settings storage on your device, for Continue and chapter selection. It is not sent off your device. |
| Collection records (picture-book pages collected, dokkaebi fragments, mini-game stars) | Kept only on your device, to show the picture book and the dokkaebi collection. |
| Text size, text speed, skipping waiting scenes, and background music and sound effect settings | Kept only on your device, to preserve your settings. |
| Your email address and the contents of your inquiry | Processed to reply to and resolve your inquiry if you contact us by email. |

## 2. Permissions and Device Features

On both Android and iOS, the app does not use internet access and does not request permissions that need separate consent, such as camera, location, photos, contacts or microphone. In the mini-game played by tilting the device, readings from the device's motion sensors are used only to control the game; they are not stored or sent anywhere.

## 3. Retention and Deletion

1. You can remove the information kept on your device by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Copies made by your operating system's backup and restore features are kept and deleted according to that operating system's settings, so uninstalling the app does not by itself delete backup copies.
2. Inquiry emails are kept for one year to reply to and resolve the inquiry, and then deleted. If a law requires us to keep them longer, we follow that period. Please do not include personal information that your inquiry does not need, or other people's information.
