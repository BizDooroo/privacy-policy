**Bizdooroo** (the “Developer,” “we,” or “us”) explains how **Dangerous Commute (위험한 출근길)** (the “Service”) handles information below.

## 1. The Service and Information Handling

The Service is a game that requires no registration or login. It processes location and motion sensor information on your device to calculate distance travelled and drink movement, and stores game records on your device. Under the current default distribution configuration, external crash reporting is disabled, and the app does not automatically send location, sensor information, or game records to the Developer or external servers. Sharing you choose to initiate and emails you separately send to support are handled under Sections 4 and 6.

## 2. Information Processed on Your Device and Its Purposes

| Information | Processing and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Location coordinates | Used temporarily to calculate distance during gameplay. Coordinates and travel routes are not stored. |
| Motion sensor information, including acceleration and rotation | Used to calculate drink movement and device orientation in real time. Raw sensor readings are not stored as a history. |
| Commute start/end times, end reason, distance, remaining drink, and distance/drink measurements over time | Stored in the device database to display commute records and result graphs. |
| Drink, appearance, game mode, objectives and results, calibration status, and measurement-quality information such as missing readings | Stored on the device to interpret and reproduce game results. |
| Earned stamps, selected objective/appearance/drink, haptic and reduced-motion preferences, and tutorial/notice acknowledgment state | Stored on the device to preserve collection progress and preferences. |

## 3. Permissions and Withdrawal

- **Location (while using the app):** Measures distance during a commute game. Measurement continues with the screen off while the game you started is running, and stops when the game ends. Android uses a location foreground service; iOS uses background location functionality. The app does not request “Always” permission. You can play without distance measurement if you do not grant location access.
- **Notifications (Android 13 and later):** Displays the ongoing-game notification. Manage this through your operating system's notification settings.
- **Add to Photo Library (iOS):** Used when you save a result card to Photos from the share sheet. This permission is not used to read or upload existing photos.

You can revoke permissions in device settings. Revocation limits features that need those permissions but does not delete records or copies already shared.

## 4. Sharing and External Services

When you choose to share, a result-card image, including remaining drink, distance, duration, date, and graph, is passed to the app you select. A temporary image may be created in your device's cache for sharing. Copies in the receiving app or photo library must be deleted separately there. Subsequent processing is governed by that service's policy.

Under the current default distribution configuration, the app does not transmit information for advertising, tracking, or external analytics, and does not use server storage, outsourced processing, or international transfers of game information. If information transmission such as external crash reporting is introduced, we will update this policy with the information involved, providers, retention periods, and user choices, and provide any required notice and consent process.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Game records and preferences remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting a commute record removes that record and its graph data, while earned stamps and separate preferences remain.

To remove all on-device information, clear app data where the operating system supports it or delete the app. Offloading an app on iOS may retain its data and is different from deleting it. Operating-system backup and restore copies and shared images must be managed separately; deleting the app does not by itself delete those copies.

## 6. Support Information and Your Rights

If you contact us by email, we process your sender email address, message, and attachments only to respond and resolve your inquiry. Under the existing policy, we retain this information for one year and then delete it. Contacting support is optional and is not required to use the app. Please do not send unnecessary personal information or information about other people. Email providers are used to transmit and store correspondence.

You may contact us below to request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of processing of your inquiry information, or to withdraw consent. A legal guardian or authorized representative may also make a request; we verify their authority only to the extent necessary. Provide only the minimum information needed to locate the inquiry, such as the sender email address and approximate date. We explain the outcome or any applicable limitations in accordance with applicable law. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete emails you have already sent.

We cannot remotely access, correct, or delete information stored only on your device. Please manage it through the app and operating-system controls. We delete inquiry information when it is no longer needed; if retention is legally required, we retain it on the applicable legal basis for the required period.
