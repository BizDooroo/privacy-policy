**비즈두루(Bizdooroo)** (the "Developer") discloses the information processed by **체스리가도** (the "Service") and the purposes for which it is used, as follows.

## 1. Service and Information Processing

The Service is a chess learning app where you can learn chess rules and tactics, play against an AI on your device, and play online against other users. It has no account sign-up or login feature and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number. Learning and AI games are processed on the device and do not require communication with a server. Information is sent off the device only when you use online play or when an app error is reported, as described below.

| Item Processed | Where It Is Processed and Why |
| --- | --- |
| Puzzle and lesson completion records, language setting | Stored only in the device's settings storage to keep your learning progress and display language. They are not sent to a server. |
| Room code, random participant identifier issued by the server, game identifier, moves and game state | Exchanged with the server to connect opponents and run games in online play (creating a room, joining with a room code, random matchmaking). |
| Error reports, such as error type and message, stack information, app version, and device and operating system information | Sent to the error reporting service to identify and fix app errors. |
| Email address and content of inquiries | Processed to reply and resolve issues when a user sends an inquiry by email. |

The Service has no advertising, usage analytics, or in-app purchase features. It does not sell user information or track users for advertising purposes.

## 2. Online Play and Server Communication

When you choose online play, the app communicates over HTTPS with a game server operated by the Developer (Amazon Web Services, Seoul Region). To connect opponents and run games, the server processes the room code, a randomly issued participant identifier, the game identifier, and moves. Online play has no feature for entering a name or nickname. During communication, AWS may process connection information such as IP addresses to deliver requests. The game server does not store this information or use it to tell users apart.

Anyone who receives a room code can join that room, so share it only with the person you want to play with.

## 3. Error Reporting

To fix errors, the app uses the error reporting feature of Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), and reports are sent to Sentry servers in the United States. It is configured to send only error and crash information. It does not send screenshots, screen recordings, usage sessions, performance traces, logs, or user information. The same error is sent no more than 3 times during a single run of the app. Information processed by Sentry is subject to the [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

## 4. Notification Permission

The app asks for notification permission when you create a room or start random matchmaking in online play. This permission is used only to show a notification on the device when a game starts while the app is in the background. No external server is used to send notifications. You can still use learning, AI games, and online play if you do not grant the permission. The app does not ask for any other device permissions, such as camera, location, contacts, or photos.

## 5. Retention Period and Deletion

1. Learning records and the language setting stored on the device can be removed by clearing the app's data or deleting the app. Copies made by the operating system's backup and restore features follow that operating system's settings.
2. Online play information (room code, participant and game identifiers, moves) is used only to run games. It is automatically deleted from the server 15 minutes after its last update (2 minutes for rooms waiting for an opponent and for random matchmaking). Deleting a room deletes it immediately. Server error logs are kept for 7 days and then deleted.
3. Error information in Sentry is deleted after no more than 90 days, according to that service's retention policy and the project settings.
4. Inquiry emails are kept for 1 year to reply and resolve issues, and are then deleted. If the law requires a longer retention period, that period applies.
